## Scope and limitations

The values depend on the documented BHSA snapshot, text representations, and numerical conventions. They are intended for reproducible exploration and analysis. A numerical match does not, by itself, establish a historical or interpretive relationship.

One malformed source qere is explicitly unavailable; full-form values repeat on constituent word slots. Read the [representation specification](representations.md) before aggregating values or comparing readings.
